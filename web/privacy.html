Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PeekVision.io handles information when you use our browser-based vision screening tools.

PeekVision.io provides preliminary vision screening only. It is not a substitute for a comprehensive examination by an optometrist, ophthalmologist, or other qualified eye-care professional.

Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, you may choose to enter information such as your name, age, gender, vision correction status, or screening responses.

Vision screening results may include information such as visual acuity screening levels, right-eye, left-eye and both-eyes results, near or distance screening results, and color-vision screening responses.

Voice-Assisted Screening

The Accessible Vision Test may use your device or browser microphone when you choose to provide spoken letter responses.

Microphone access is only used when you activate the voice-response feature and grant browser permission. PeekVision.io does not intentionally activate the microphone without your action.

Speech-recognition functionality may depend on browser, operating-system, or third-party speech-recognition capabilities. Those services may have their own privacy practices.

Information Automatically Processed

Like most websites, hosting, security, analytics, or content-delivery providers may automatically process limited technical information such as:

  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Approximate network or IP information
  • Pages or features accessed
  • Diagnostic, security, or performance information

This information may be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.

Cookies and Local Browser Storage

PeekVision.io may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies when required for application functionality, preferences, security, or analytics.

You can control cookies and site permissions through your browser settings.

Health and Screening Information

Vision screening information generated by PeekVision.io should not be considered a medical diagnosis, medical record, or prescription.

Do not rely on an online screening result to delay professional care if you have sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, new flashes or floaters, eye injury, or other concerning symptoms.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide the requested vision screening experience
  • Display screening results and reports
  • Operate and improve website functionality
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
  • Diagnose technical problems

Sale of Personal Information

PeekVision.io does not sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

PeekVision.io may rely on third-party infrastructure such as website hosting, content-delivery networks, browser services, speech-recognition technologies, analytics, or security providers.

Information processed by those providers is subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to the technical behavior of the browser and any third-party infrastructure used by the service.

Children

PeekVision.io is a general vision-screening tool and is not specifically directed to young children. A parent, guardian, or responsible adult should supervise use by minors where appropriate.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Decline microphone permission
  • Use manual response buttons instead of voice input
  • Clear browser site data through your browser settings
  • Stop using the service at any time

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the service. However, no website, browser, transmission method, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PeekVision.io evolves. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the Contact section of PeekVision.io.
