How to Convert Snellen to logMAR

The general mathematical relationship is:

logMAR = log10(Snellen denominator ÷ Snellen numerator)

For standard U.S. 20-foot Snellen notation:

logMAR = log10(denominator ÷ 20)
Example: 20/40

logMAR = log10(40 ÷ 20)
logMAR = log10(2)
logMAR ≈ 0.30

How to Convert logMAR to Snellen

For a 20-foot Snellen numerator, the approximate denominator can be calculated from logMAR using:

Snellen denominator = 20 × 10logMAR
Example: logMAR 0.30

denominator = 20 × 100.30
denominator ≈ 40

Therefore: 0.30 logMAR ≈ 20/40

What Does logMAR Mean?

logMAR means logarithm of the Minimum Angle of Resolution .

Unlike Snellen notation, logMAR uses a logarithmic scale. This makes it useful for comparing changes in visual acuity across standardized chart levels.

  • 0.00 logMAR corresponds approximately to 20/20.
  • Positive logMAR values generally represent reduced visual acuity compared with the 20/20 reference.
  • Negative logMAR values represent acuity better than the 20/20 reference.

Snellen vs logMAR vs Decimal Acuity

Snellen

Snellen notation is widely recognized in the United States and is commonly written as values such as 20/20, 20/40 or 20/100.

logMAR

logMAR expresses visual acuity on a logarithmic scale. Standardized research and clinical charts often use logMAR because each step can represent a more consistent change in visual acuity.

Decimal acuity

Decimal acuity is another way to express visual acuity. It can be calculated approximately by dividing the Snellen numerator by the denominator.

Decimal acuity = Snellen numerator ÷ Snellen denominator

For example:

20/40 = 20 ÷ 40 = 0.50 decimal acuity

20/20 corresponds to approximately 0.00 logMAR.

What is 20/40 in logMAR?

What is 20/50 in logMAR?

20/50 corresponds to approximately 0.40 logMAR.

What is 20/100 in logMAR?

20/100 corresponds to approximately 0.70 logMAR.

Is a lower logMAR value better?

Generally, yes. Lower logMAR values indicate better visual acuity. The 20/20 reference is approximately 0.00 logMAR, while values below zero correspond to acuity better than that reference.

About Online Visual Acuity Measurements

Visual acuity measured through a web browser can be affected by physical screen size, display scaling, browser zoom, viewing distance, lighting and device settings.

For this reason, values calculated or displayed by an online screening tool should not automatically be interpreted as a clinically calibrated measurement.
